Where does Pharmopedia live?

Patient Lite Mobile currently has 4 tabs: Home, Timeline, Privacy, Notifications

A

New tab in Patient Lite Mobile

Pharmopedia becomes a 5th tab. Shares existing auth, hubFetch, offline storage (expo-sqlite), and zustand store. Lowest overhead — no new app bundle, no separate Play Store listing.

⭐ Recommended

B

Standalone Expo app

Separate app with its own auth flow, hubFetch wrapper, and offline stack. Own Play Store / App Store listing. Useful if Pharmopedia is intended for a much broader non-patient audience (general public, non-clinical staff).
