Offline Indicator
A persistent banner when offline. Search and browse work fully from local cache. Sync timestamp shown on Profile tab ("Last updated 2h ago").
RTL / Dari & Pashto
Language toggle in Profile. Dari/Pashto names shown as the primary title when the local name exists. Full RTL layout flip — same pattern as Patient Lite.
Deep Links
OPD-Lite and Pharmacy-Lite can deep-link into Pharmopedia drug detail by ATC code. Share button on drug detail generates a link for peer sharing.
