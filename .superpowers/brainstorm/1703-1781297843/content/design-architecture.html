WHO EML Seed
~500 medicines imported at setup. Provides ATC codes, INN names, standard dosage forms, and therapeutic categories. Pharmopedia's baseline content from day one.
Local Enrichment Layer
Pharmacists add Dari/Pashto names, local brand names, facility availability flags, and Afghanistan-specific notes via Pharmacy-Lite. Clinicians add clinical annotations via OPD-Lite.
Offline-First Sync
Pharmopedia downloads the full catalog to local SQLite (SQLCipher) on first login. Incremental sync via HLC watermarks — same pattern as other spokes. Drug lookups work fully offline.
