How pharmacy prices flow into Pharmopedia
Pharmacy-Lite
Pharmacist sets retail price
per drug × form × quantity
→
Hub /drug-catalog
Stores price + location
+ stock signal per pharmacy
→
Pharmopedia
Queries Hub by drug + coords
Sorts by distance or price
⚠️ Pharmacy location data requires Pharmacy-Lite facilities to have GPS coordinates stored in Hub. Price data is real-time from Hub (not offline-cached) — requires connectivity to show the pharmacy list. A "prices unavailable offline" state is shown when disconnected.
