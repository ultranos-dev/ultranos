Pharmacy Lite: Inventory & POS Architecture

Module decomposition for the full enterprise pharmacy system

System Modules

Module 1: Medication Catalog
  • Hub-managed formulary (synced to local cache)
  • Product: name, form, strength, barcode (EAN-13), category
  • Controlled substance schedule (II, III, IV, V)
  • Selling price, cost price, margin
  • Reorder point, min/max stock levels
Module 2: Stock Management
  • Stock records: catalog_id, batch, lot, expiry, qty, zone
  • Immediate local deduction on dispense
  • FEFO enforcement (First Expiry, First Out)
  • Auto-quarantine expired stock
  • Stock adjustments (damage, returns, corrections)
  • Physical count / reconciliation workflow
  • Controlled substance running balance
Module 3: Procurement
  • Supplier management (contacts, lead times, terms)
  • PO lifecycle: Draft → Sent → Partially Received → Closed
  • Goods receiving with barcode scan
  • Partial receipts supported
  • Toggle: PO mode vs simple receiving (settings)
  • Cost price captured at receipt time
Module 4: Network & Transfers
  • Stock levels sync to Hub
  • Cross-location stock visibility
  • Transfer requests between pharmacies
  • Transfer states: Requested → Shipped → Received
  • Org-level consolidated stock view (Hub/Admin)
Module 5: Point of Sale
  • Invoice generation (line items from dispense)
  • Payment: cash, card, credit/tab
  • Patient credit ledger + aging reports
  • Daily cash drawer reconciliation
  • Receipt printing (PDF / thermal)
  • Tax field (configurable per country)
Module 6: Reporting & Alerts
  • Dashboard: stock value, low-stock, near-expiry
  • Consumption trends (fast/slow movers)
  • Controlled substance discrepancy report
  • Wastage rate (expired + damaged)
  • Financial: daily sales, margins, receivables
  • Near-expiry alerts (configurable threshold)
  • Low-stock alerts (at reorder point)
