Banner Stacking Order

Safety-critical banners sit at the top of the page, uncollapsible. Which priority order feels right?

A

Safety-First (Recommended)

Follows CLAUDE.md Rule #4 strictly — life-threatening info first, operational info last.

🔴 Allergy Banner — always visible, never collapsed
🟡 Sync Conflict Banner — Tier 1 safety conflicts block prescriptions
🟡 MPI Duplicate Warning — wrong-patient risk
🟡 Consent Expiry Alert — data access at risk
🔵 Biometric Stale — informational, non-urgent
B

Clinical-Then-Operational

Groups clinical safety together, then operational/identity concerns.

🔴 Allergy Banner
🟡 Sync Conflict Banner
🔵 Biometric Stale
🟡 MPI Duplicate Warning
🟡 Consent Expiry Alert

Note: Most patients will show zero banners. The stack only appears when something needs attention. Allergy "No Known Allergies" always shows (gray, not red).
